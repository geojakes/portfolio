About Me

I'm an engineering leader based in Waterloo, Canada, currently a Senior Engineering Manager at Faire. I'm deeply passionate about developer productivity at scale — the idea that the right tools, infrastructure, and teams can unlock an entire organization's potential.

Whether it's architecting autonomous coding agents that reshape engineering workflows, leading the rollout of cloud environments that make onboarding instant, or setting the strategy that steers how an org adopts AI tooling — I'm happiest when I'm removing friction and making complex things feel simple. The throughline across my work has stayed the same as I've grown from hands-on platform engineer to engineering manager: developer productivity at scale.

I hold an M.A.Sc. from the University of Waterloo and a B.Tech from NIT Calicut. That research background taught me to dig deep into problems, while building at scale at Faire and Magnet Forensics taught me to ship with impact — and now to scale that impact through teams and strategy.
